Privacy policy

Last updated September 11, 2026

This page explains what SpamViking collects, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, the name you choose for your host, and your mobile number if you opt in to texts.
  • Material you hand over — the unsolicited messages and calls you choose to give to SpamViking, including the sender's contact details.
  • Interaction records — recordings, transcripts, and message threads from interactions the service takes part in.
  • Usage data — timing, duration, and outcome of interactions, and delivery records for any texts we send you.

How we use it

  • To provide and improve the service, including to notify you.
  • To publish excerpts of interactions. Before publishing, we remove your name, email, and phone number.
  • To understand the unsolicited contact you receive, which may include consulting publicly available information.

Text messages

Your mobile number is used only to send the alerts you opted in to. We do not share it with third parties for their marketing, and mobile opt-in data is never sold. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run SpamViking, such as hosting, messaging, and AI providers. They process data on our behalf and under our instructions. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Recordings are kept for a limited period unless they are part of published content. Account details and material you hand over are kept while your account is active. Text-delivery records are kept for compliance purposes.

Your choices

  • Turn texts off by replying STOP or in your settings.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data by emailing raid@spamviking.com.
  • Ask us to take down published content that includes you.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top tells you when.
