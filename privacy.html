Privacy policy

Last updated September 11, 2026

This page explains what SpamViking collects, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, the host name you choose, and your mobile number if you opt in to texts.
  • Scam material you forward — emails, text messages, and voicemails, including the sender's address or phone number.
  • Calls and conversations — recordings and transcripts of calls our host takes part in, and the email threads it conducts. Every call includes a recording notice.
  • Usage data — call times, durations, and outcomes, and delivery records for any texts we send you.

How we use it

  • To run the service: reply to scammers, book and place calls, and notify you.
  • To improve the host — we use recordings and transcripts to make it better at what it does.
  • To publish comedy clips. Before publishing, we remove your name, email, and phone number. The scammer's side of a conversation may be published.
  • To look up scammer phone numbers and addresses on public complaint sites so the host knows what script to expect.

Text messages

Your mobile number is used only to send the alerts you opted in to. We do not share it with third parties for marketing, and mobile opt-in data is never sold. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run SpamViking — hosting, telephony, SMS delivery, speech-to-text, and AI models. They process data on our behalf and under our instructions. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Call recordings are kept for 30 days unless they are part of a published clip. Account details and scam material are kept while your account is active. Text-delivery records are kept for compliance purposes.

Your choices

  • Turn texts off by replying STOP or in your settings.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data by emailing raid@spamviking.com.
  • Ask us to take down a published clip that includes you.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top tells you when.
